*Electrostatic precipitator, PM sensors* · Design Thinking course project, IIT Jammu · Aug 2026 – present

## The problem

I interviewed small-factory (MSME) owners about why pollution control is hard to adopt, and scoped the product around **servicing**.

## The design

An electrostatic precipitator (ESP) packaged as a **slide-out cassette**. It holds the plates, electrodes, insulators and cleaning interface, so it can be cleaned and swapped without dismantling the unit.

## The prototype

I built the prototype and tested it with controlled smoke. Air passes through this chain:

> pre-filter → pre-separator → flow straightener → **ESP cassette** → activated carbon → fine filter → 12 V blower

PM sensors at the inlet and outlet were compared across blower speeds.
